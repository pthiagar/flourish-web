2004 – 2009 Cycle

The 2008 Financial Crisis

Surviving a -57% Market Crash and Frozen Bank Lending

Stock Market: -57% Bank Lending: Frozen Interest Rates: Cut to 0%
What Happened

When bank lending froze in 2008, investors who relied on short-term, adjustable loans lost their properties overnight. Financial tricks failed when credit dried up.

While luxury developments fell into foreclosure, our working-class rental housing maintained 90%+ occupancy. People always need an affordable place to live.

Rules We Follow
  • 1. Budget for Real Expenses: Assume at least 50% of rental income goes toward maintenance and operations, ignoring optimistic sales pitches.
  • 2. Fixed-Rate Loans Only: Lock in 7 to 10-year fixed mortgages. Never use short-term adjustable loans.
  • 3. 12-Month Cash Buffer: Keep a full year of mortgage, tax, and insurance payments in cash reserves.
Result: 100% of mortgage payments made on time with zero emergency cash requested from investors. 100% Solvency
2010 – 2019 Cycle

The Low-Interest Era & Tech Boom

Disciplined Investing When Money Was Cheap

Interest Rates: Near Zero S&P 500: +13.6% / Year Startup Valuations: Extreme Highs
What Happened

A decade of near-zero interest rates made investors careless. In tech, investors overpaid for unprofitable startups. In real estate, buyers paid record prices assuming rents would rise forever.

We avoided bidding wars, working directly with long-term owners to buy properties well below what they would cost to build today, while staying disciplined on early-stage startup investments.

Rules We Follow
  • 1. Direct Deals Only: Buy directly from long-term owners to avoid overpaying in broker auctions.
  • 2. Startup Ownership Protection: Secure contractual rights to maintain our equity share as companies grow.
  • 3. Buy Below Construction Cost: Never pay more for a property than the cost of building it brand new.
Result: Acquired properties at an average 28% discount to construction costs while avoiding overpriced tech rounds. Disciplined Entry
2020 – 2021 Cycle

The 2020 Market Shock & Rebound

Protecting Downside and Buying During Market Panic

Fastest -30% Drop (22 Days) Market Volatility: Historic Spike Government Stimulus: $4.8T
What Happened

In March 2020, markets suffered the fastest 30% collapse in history. Panicked investors paid exorbitant prices trying to buy last-minute portfolio insurance.

Because we routinely buy inexpensive downside insurance during calm markets, our hedges surged in value. We took profits as planned and reinvested the cash into discounted quality assets.

Rules We Follow
  • 1. Buy Insurance When Cheap: Budget 1% for downside protection during calm periods—never buy insurance during a panic.
  • 2. Pre-Set Profit Targets: Systematically cash out half of protective positions when they gain 5x to 10x to lock in returns.
  • 3. Reinvest in Quality: Direct crash profits into short-term government bonds and discounted high-quality investments.
Result: Downside hedges gained over 2,000% in March 2020, generating cash to buy quality assets at fire-sale prices. +2,000% Gain
2022 – Present (Current Cycle)

The Rapid Rate Hikes & Valuation Reset

Generating Reliable Cash Flow When Interest Rates Rose

Interest Rate Hikes: +5.25% Traditional Portfolios: -16.9% Cash Yields: 5%+
What Happened

The fastest interest rate hikes in 40 years hit traditional stock-and-bond portfolios simultaneously. Property operators with adjustable-rate loans lost buildings to lenders when payments skyrocketed.

We protected our capital by avoiding adjustable debt and using conservative options strategies to generate consistent monthly cash yield from market volatility.

Rules We Follow
  • 1. Systematic Cash-Flow Strategies: Sell conservative options against quality holdings to generate steady monthly income.
  • 2. Higher Rate Stress-Tests: Verify every property purchase remains profitable even if interest rates stay high and property values drop.
  • 3. Founder Execution Speed: In startups, evaluate how fast teams build and retain paying customers, not media hype.
Result: Produced 14.2% annualized cash yield with less downside fluctuation than the broader market. 14.2% Annual Cash Yield
Our Investment Committee applies these rules to every investment. No speculation. No unhedged debt.
